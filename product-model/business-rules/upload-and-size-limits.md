# Photos, files, counts and matches have fixed limits

A task holds up to 6 photos of up to 25 MB each in PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF; an appliance file is up to 100 MB; a counted wear item targets at most 250 uses; a declarative companion matches at most 500 entities.
